# gottheimer

THE NEWS: On Friday, September 18, 2026, U.S. Rep. Josh Gottheimer (D-NJ-5) introduced two bipartisan AI security bills at Fairleigh Dickinson University in Teaneck, NJ: the American AI Security Act, which would require mandatory NSA reviews of the most powerful AI models before release, and the China FIREWALL Act, which would ban Chinese-developed AI from all federal government systems and devices.
