## Caption

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said in Politico's new Decoded interview that the world should accept some bad things happening for the benefits of AI, and he named that as a point where he differs from Anthropic and its CEO Dario Amodei.

Altman said he would not take a trade guaranteeing no major hacks, no misuse and zero scams, because he thinks people will do orders of magnitude more good than bad. He also said he does not accept the really catastrophic risks, including a serious loss of control to AI. Zscaler CEO Jay Chaudhry said on Yahoo Finance that Altman is wrong. In an earlier Fortune interview, Altman called a 10% risk of AI killing everyone by the end of the decade unacceptable.
