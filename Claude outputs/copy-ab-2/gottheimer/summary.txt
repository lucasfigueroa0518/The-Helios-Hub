## CAPTION
Rep. Josh Gottheimer introduced two bipartisan bills Friday that would require the NSA to review powerful AI models before release and ban Chinese-developed models from federal devices.

The American AI Security Act would mandate that developers give the NSA full access to check whether a model could enable cyberattacks or help build chemical, biological, or radiological weapons. The NSA would have 30 days to decide, with one possible extension. Right now, such reviews are voluntary under a June 2026 executive order. The China FIREWALL Act would expand an existing DeepSeek ban to cover other Chinese models like Moonshot's Kimi K3 and Alibaba's Qwen that are still used on federal systems.

Gottheimer acknowledged the bills face a steep climb: Republican leadership has refused to bring AI safety legislation to a vote, though he called on the Speaker to reconvene Congress before the election to pass them.
