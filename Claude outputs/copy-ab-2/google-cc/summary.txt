## CAPTION
Google Labs is turning CC, its experimental AI agent, into a shared household tool. Up to six family members can now use one CC account together, each choosing what information to share, emails, calendar events, photos, Drive folders, so CC can manage the family's schedules, tasks, and logistics. Every morning it delivers "Your Day Ahead," a briefing of who needs to be where and what's pending. CC can also fill out permission slips, draft meal plans, check drive times between activities, and update a shared family calendar. The catch: it's U.S.-only for now, limited to people 18 and over with personal Gmail accounts, which means school-provided accounts don't work and tweens and teens cannot use it.
