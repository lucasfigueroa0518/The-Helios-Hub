## Focal (V-5) · HARD CONSTRAINT

Each beat has exactly one focal entity: the thing the viewer must look at. It is the only element in the
orange family (the opener title's concept word is outside the world and does not count), with one
exception: **the key item keeps its orange wherever it appears** (the anchor block's `hero:`, an object,
in its accent part). A viewer follows the story by its key item, so its look never changes. **No critter
is ever orange**, and no critter's body, wear or color ever changes during the reel (world.js `CAST`,
checked by `try.mjs`). When a beat's focal is another entity, it takes orange too, and that is the only
time two oranges share the screen.
It dominates its nearest neighbor by 3× or more in size, contrast, or motion. Camera and motion deliver
the eye to it within 0.5s of the beat's start. The focal changes only at a beat boundary or on the step
the voice is naming.
