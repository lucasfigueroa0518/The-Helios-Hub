Composition: the photograph is the slide. One 2px plumb line on the outer margin runs from a canvas edge to the caption and thickens to an 8px bar exactly along the headline, so the type reads as a label hung on the picture, not a card laid over it. The scrim is built to the caption box: solid under the text, falling away above it, and fading out sideways so the far side of the scene is untouched.

Eye path: Entry = the open scene (upper right, nothing competes). Read = the headline, reached by dropping down the rule. Settle = the small caption line under it, which opens the loop.

Orange moment: only the headline's hook span; the rule, bar and arrow stay in narrative ink.

Spreads: rails sit on the outer margins (left edge of the left half, right edge of the right half) with right-aligned type on the right half, so the pair reads as one picture with two captions. The scrim fades to zero at the seam so the photo joins perfectly. The left half carries one Lucide arrow riding the seam edge; the right half's left edge is left empty.

Why more premium than title-over-photo: the type is architecture (rail, bar, measure) tied to the margin and the frame edge, the photo stays whole, and nothing is boxed.
