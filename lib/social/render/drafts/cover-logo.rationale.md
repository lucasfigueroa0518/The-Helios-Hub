# Cover · logo — The plinth

Composition: the company is a slab. A cream or charcoal plate (chosen by code) is bolted into the bottom-right of the canvas, starting on the 96px margin and bleeding off the right and bottom edges, with one soft corner. The logo sits inside it at a confident size (width-led at about 80% when wide). The claim is set at full cover scale on the black above, bottom-anchored so its last baseline stands on the slab edge.

Eye path: Entry = the slab and logo (biggest, highest-contrast shape, centre of the grid crop). Read = the claim climbing the black field above it. Settle = the swipe arrow on the slab's top edge, right margin.

Orange moment: the headline's hook span only. Nothing else is orange.

Why it is more premium: the plate is architecture, not a card. It has an offset origin, two bleeds, one radius, and a vertical HELIOS spine in the gutter it leaves behind. Type and logo never overlap, so contrast holds on all four canvases and both plate colours.
