**Composition.** The number is the whole slide: set at ~400px with tight tracking so "$2B" spans the full 888px content width, tabular figures, no decoration. Beneath it runs a measuring scale (hairline ruler, ticks, end caps) that carries the number note as its caption, as if the figure were read off an instrument. The label (headline) sits small and cold top-left; the context sentence is parked at the foot across a deliberate wide gap.

**Eye path.**
- Entry: the numeral, centred in the Instagram grid crop (y ~48%).
- Read: the scale caption, which says what is being counted (y ~69%).
- Settle: the one-line context at the foot, left-aligned (y ~89%).

**Orange moment.** Whatever the span roles say; the slide adds no colour of its own. The numeral is narrative white unless the writer marks it hook, and then the whole figure is the single orange moment.

**Why it is more premium.** The scale is the one idea: the number is measured, not captioned, so the note has a reason to sit under it. Long figures shrink to fit width (floor 52) instead of wrapping. With a photo, the same type sits on a vertical scrim over the Backdrop; without, the figure carries the slide alone.
