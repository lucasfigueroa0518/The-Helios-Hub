Split stat: two counts on one measuring line.

Composition: a hairline axis runs down the left gutter with a tick at each number's baseline. The first
count sits small and white (about 170px); the second runs about 2.2x its size (about 375px), nearly edge
to edge, so the ratio between the two numbers is felt before it is read. Notes hang off each number like
axis labels. The framing headline and context line anchor the foot.

Eye path: Entry = the small first number (top-left, reading order). Read = the giant second number,
reached by dropping down the axis. Settle = headline and body at the foot, below the grid-crop band.

Orange moment: the second number only. Hook spans in title, headline and body render as narrative here
so nothing competes. On green the number is ink; on orange it is orange on a full-bleed ink slab (the
hook-chip treatment); on white and black it is brand orange.

Why more premium than a stat card: the contrast is scale itself, not a box, a divider, or a chart. No
delta is invented; the length of the axis is the implied gap.
