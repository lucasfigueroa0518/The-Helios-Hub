Quote family (speaker, backdrop, type). The words are the slide.
Composition: an oversized serif opening mark hangs at the left margin; a single vertical hairline falls from it,
the quote sits against that hairline at the largest size its box allows (148px for a short line, fitting down
toward 44px for a long one), and the hairline ends at a short green rule and the mono attribution.
The framing headline stays small at the top-left, clearly under the quote.
Eye path: Entry the mark and first word (left, upper third) / Read the quote down the hairline (centre, y 54%)
/ Settle the mono name and role (left, y 77%). The hook word and quote sit inside the 3:4 grid crop.
Speaker mode: same hairline structure; the verified face is its own tall, narrow column (about 296px x 700px,
square corners) on the right, the quote wraps beside it, the face is never under text.
Backdrop mode: a photo as mood only, with a soft vertical scrim band behind the quote. Type mode: role follows the name.
One orange moment: the hook word the data already carries (the mark only turns orange when the quote has no hook).
Why it is more premium than title-over-photo: scale contrast plus one structural line, no frames, no cards.
