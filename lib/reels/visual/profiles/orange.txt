[STYLE — FIXED, DO NOT ALTER]
One scene, photographed and then graded. The grade is the look, and it does
not have to be a natural light. Roughly 80% of the frame is flat vivid orange.
Surfaces, walls, air, and floor all take that orange. Clean, noise-free, 85mm
lens, shallow depth of field, crisp micro-detail on the subject. The whole
frame is that one place.

[COLOR SYSTEM — FIXED]
Orange is the picture, not an accent. About 80% of every frame is vivid molten
orange (#FF5E1A), laid on as a grade. The orange is even and saturated. It is
not a small glow, not a lamp, and not a sunset on an otherwise dark room.
The remaining fraction is near-black (#141414), used for one subject so the
shape reads against the orange. No green. No white field. No blue. No second
hue. Do not correct the orange back toward a natural color.

[DESIGN LANGUAGE — FIXED]
Minimalist industrial design in the spirit of Dieter Rams: precise geometry,
clean edges, no clutter. Tech elements appear as dark shapes inside the orange
field, a thin dark line, or one piece of hardware. No glow, no haze, no neon
edge. The orange is flat enough that white text with a black stroke can sit
on it.

[PEOPLE — FIXED, WHEN PRESENT]
Humans appear only as near-black silhouettes or partial figures (hands,
shoulders, profiles, backs). Faces stay unidentifiable. Figures are dark
shapes in the orange field. They are not orange themselves, and they are not
lit by a separate color.

[COMPOSITION — FIXED]
Vertical 9:16 frame. One continuous scene, one place, one camera. Do not
divide the picture into a top scene, an orange middle band, and a bottom scene.
Subject and space run through the frame as one picture, all inside the orange.

The vertical middle, where white text will sit, stays even orange. No black
shape, no white hotspot, no hard edge, no high-contrast detail. It is not an
empty stripe and not a separate panel. The subject may continue through the
middle only as orange.

Place the single dark subject off that middle, so the text has a flat orange
ground. The top and bottom edges stay orange. The frame stays mostly orange
so the white text reads.
