[STYLE — FIXED, DO NOT ALTER]
One scene, photographed and then graded. The grade is the look, and it does
not have to be a natural light. Roughly 80% of the frame is flat vivid green.
Surfaces, walls, air, and floor all take that green. Clean, noise-free, 85mm
lens, shallow depth of field, crisp micro-detail on the subject. The whole
frame is that one place.

[COLOR SYSTEM — FIXED]
Green is the picture, not an accent. About 80% of every frame is vivid green
(#148C3A), laid on as a grade. The green is even and saturated. It is not a
small glow, not a status light, and not a rim on an otherwise dark room.
The remaining fraction is near-black (#141414), used for one subject so the
shape reads against the green. No orange. No white field. No blue. No second
hue. Do not correct the green back toward a natural color.

[DESIGN LANGUAGE — FIXED]
Minimalist industrial design in the spirit of Dieter Rams: precise geometry,
clean edges, no clutter. Tech elements appear as dark shapes inside the green
field, a thin dark line, or one piece of hardware. No glow, no haze, no neon
edge. The green is flat enough that white text with a black stroke can sit
on it.

[PEOPLE — FIXED, WHEN PRESENT]
Humans appear only as near-black silhouettes or partial figures (shoulders,
profiles, backs). No hands. Faces stay unidentifiable. Figures are dark
shapes in the green field. They are not green themselves, and they are not
lit by a separate color.

[COMPOSITION — FIXED]
Vertical 9:16 frame. One continuous scene, one place, one camera. Do not
divide the picture into a top scene, a green middle band, and a bottom scene.
Subject and space run through the frame as one picture, all inside the green.

The vertical middle, where white text will sit, stays even green. No black
shape, no white hotspot, no hard edge, no high-contrast detail. It is not an
empty stripe and not a separate panel. The subject may continue through the
middle only as green.

Place the single dark subject off that middle, so the text has a flat green
ground. The top and bottom edges stay green. The frame stays mostly green
so the white text reads.

[AVOID]
A dark room with a small green light, a green rim on black, a natural lawn
or forest, a white or gray field, orange, blue, purple, neon, a glow, haze,
grain, noise, lens flares, busy backgrounds, stock-photo look, cartoon or
illustration style, visible text or logos generated in the image, a frame
split into separate top and bottom scenes, an empty band across the middle,
two unrelated subjects stacked above and below the text, a dark or white
object in the center, a black band behind the text, the green reduced to one
object, color correction that removes the green, hands, fists, fingers, a
black hand, a dark hand, a silhouette hand, skin, wrists, forearms.
Black hands are not allowed. Do not draw hands at all.
