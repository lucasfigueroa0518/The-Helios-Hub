[STYLE — FIXED, DO NOT ALTER]
One photoreal scene, as if photographed in a real place with a real camera.
Physically based materials. High-key lighting. Matte white and warm off-white
surfaces, soft and powdery, with no shine. Roughly 85% of the frame is white
or near-white. One soft, even wash of light. No hard shadow. Clean, noise-free,
85mm lens, shallow depth of field, crisp micro-detail on the subject. The
whole frame is that one place.

[COLOR SYSTEM — FIXED]
White dominates. The field is white, ivory, and pale warm gray. Exactly one
accent, and it is small: charcoal (#1A1A1A), used for one object or one edge
that is not in the vertical middle. No orange. No green. No second hue. The
grade stays white. Nothing is blown into a featureless hole, and nothing is
pushed back toward a dark room.

[DESIGN LANGUAGE — FIXED]
Minimalist industrial design in the spirit of Dieter Rams: precise geometry,
clean edges, no clutter. Tech elements appear as thin charcoal lines on the
white field, a faint diagram, or one piece of matte hardware. The air is clear.
No haze, no glow.

[PEOPLE — FIXED, WHEN PRESENT]
Humans appear only as pale silhouettes or partial figures (hands, shoulders,
profiles, backs). Faces stay unidentifiable. Figures are the same white world
as the room, edged in soft gray. They are not dark cutouts.

[COMPOSITION — FIXED]
Vertical 9:16 frame. One continuous scene, one place, one camera. Do not
divide the picture into a top scene, a white middle band, and a bottom scene.
Subject, light, and space run through the frame as one picture.

The vertical middle, where black text will sit, stays bright and quiet. That
brightness is the same white field: pale forms, no charcoal, no hard edge, no
shadow, no high-contrast detail. It is not an empty stripe and not a separate
panel. The subject and the room may continue through the middle when that
part stays pale.

Place the single charcoal accent and the darkest part of the subject off that
middle, so the text has a white ground. The top and bottom edges stay white
and quiet. The image overall stays predominantly white so the black text reads.
