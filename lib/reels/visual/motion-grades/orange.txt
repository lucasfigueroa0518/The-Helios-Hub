GRADE: ORANGE
This clip is flooded with flat, vivid orange: about 80% of the frame is the orange grade, with one near-black subject. White text with a black stroke sits in the band, so the band stays flat, even orange on every frame: no dark shape, no white hotspot, and no hard edge.

This grade has no glow and no haze. The orange is a grade, not a light, so it never brightens, dims, flickers, or shifts in color.
- The near-black subject sits above or below the band and stays there. Its movement is the story beat, and it carries the polarity.
- The ambient detail is small and dark: a thin dark line or a loose part of the subject moving slightly. The orange field itself holds still.
