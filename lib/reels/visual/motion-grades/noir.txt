GRADE: NOIR
This clip is the dark room: about 85% of the frame in shadow, one orange focal light, green rim light and faint green glow, light haze. White text sits in the band, so the band stays deep black on every frame: unlit dark form and shade, with no light source, no highlight, no orange, and no high-contrast detail.

In this grade the light is the main thing that moves, and it moves on purpose.
- The orange light is the focal point and marks the story's turn. It sits above or below the band and stays there. Its brightness carries the polarity. It can gather from dim toward the beat and land brighter than it started, or swell once at the beat and then run down dimmer than it started, for an ending or a loss. Either way it rises quickly and settles slowly. It grows brighter, never larger, so its halo never spreads toward the band. It never pulses, breathes, or flickers on a loop.
- Green is rim light, a faint background glow, and small status lights. It follows the orange and never leads it: it answers the beat a moment later and weaker, or it holds steady. It is never brighter than the orange.
- Haze and dust are the ambient detail. They drift slowly through light that is already there, above or below the band, and they settle last. Wherever haze crosses the band, it stays unlit and dark.
- Silhouettes stay dark shapes, faces in shadow, the whole time.
