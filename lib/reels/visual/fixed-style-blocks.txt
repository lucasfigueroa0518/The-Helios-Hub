[STYLE — FIXED, DO NOT ALTER]
One photoreal scene, as if photographed in a real place with a real camera. 
Physically based materials. Low-key lighting with deep crushed blacks. Matte 
black environment with light-absorbing, soft-touch and powder-coated surfaces. 
Roughly 85% of the frame in shadow. Single motivated key light plus subtle rim 
light. Clean, noise-free, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, crisp micro-detail 
on the subject. The whole frame is that one place.

[COLOR SYSTEM — FIXED]
Monochrome matte-black and charcoal base with exactly two isolated accent colors:
- PRIMARY accent: vivid molten orange (#FF5E1A). Used for the key light, the single 
  hero glow, and the main focal point. One orange focal point per frame.
- SECONDARY accent: deep saturated emissive emerald-green (#138510). Used only for rim 
  light, faint background glow, and small UI details. Always subordinate to orange.
No other hues. The grade stays two-tone. Glows are soft, controlled, and precise, 
never blown out.

[DESIGN LANGUAGE — FIXED]
Minimalist industrial design in the spirit of Dieter Rams: precise geometry, 
clean edges, no clutter. Tech elements appear as thin-line dark-mode interface 
graphics, subtle holographic data, node networks, or minimal hardware. 
Restrained neo-noir atmosphere with light volumetric haze for depth.

[PEOPLE — FIXED, WHEN PRESENT]
Humans appear only as silhouettes or partial figures (hands, shoulders, profiles, 
backs). Faces always in shadow and never fully lit or identifiable. Figures are 
rim-lit in green or edge-lit in orange. They belong to the same real scene as 
the room around them.

[COMPOSITION — FIXED]
Vertical 9:16 frame. One continuous scene, one place, one camera. Do not 
divide the picture into a top scene, a dark middle band, and a bottom scene. 
Subject, light, and space run through the frame as one picture.

The vertical middle, where white text will sit, stays in low exposure. That 
darkness is shadow inside this same scene: dark forms, deep shade, no light 
source, no bright highlight, no orange, no high-contrast detail. It is not 
an empty stripe and not a separate panel. The subject and the room may 
continue through the middle when that part is in shadow.

Place the single orange focal point and the brightest part of the subject 
off that middle, so the text has a dark ground. Do not park one object above 
a black gap and another object below it. The top and bottom edges stay dark 
and quiet. The image overall stays predominantly dark so the text reads.
