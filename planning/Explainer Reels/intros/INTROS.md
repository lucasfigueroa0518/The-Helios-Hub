# AI Brain Break: 10 experimental openers (batch 1, 2026-10-19)

Every opener is built on the same reel, the agent-loop reel from 2026-10-14: the frosty room, the shivering thermostat critter, its real opener line ("In the next 30 seconds, this frosty room will teach you an AI concept", 20% faster) and its funk bed. That way the only thing that changes between them is the opener.

Each opener has two parts:
- an **audio pattern break**: a sound that breaks what the ear expects;
- a **visual pattern break**: a frame that breaks what the eye expects.

After the break, the scene and the "AI Brain Break" / term text settle into the reel. For each opener:
- **Text** is how "AI Brain Break" and the term are shown.
- **After** is what's on screen once the break is over.

| # | Name | First second | Audio break | Visual break | Text | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ✅ | **Paper Rip** | A full-screen sheet with "AI BRAIN BREAK" and "agent loop" hand-lettered on it. It bulges, then rips down the middle. | Quiet creak → a loud paper rip + a sub hit; the beat drops on the tear. | The halves fly off left and right, the room is revealed mid-shiver behind them. | Printed on the sheet, then carried into the top band. | The room, the line running. |
| 2 ✅ | **Record Scratch Freeze** | The room in full motion, music loud. Then everything freezes. | A record scratch; the music cuts dead; the voice starts in silence. | The frame freezes and goes ink-only; a red marker circles the critter with an arrow. | "AI BRAIN BREAK" slaps on as a sticker; the term in red marker. | Unfreezes on "this frosty room": colour and music return with a hit. |
| 3 | **Stamp Slam** | 0.15s of black and silence, then a giant rubber stamp crashes down. | Dead air → stamp SLAM + bass hit; the beat drops on the slam. | Camera shake, ink splatter, then a fast zoom through the stamped "O" into the room. | Stamped in orange ink, tilted, filling the frame. | The room; the term writes in the top band. |

**Lucas's shortlist (2026-10-19):** ✅ 1 Paper Rip, 2 Record Scratch Freeze, 4 Typewriter, 6 Big Bang, 7 Knock Knock. Not kept: 3, 5, 8, 9, 10.

**What varies across the 10:**
- **How long the break is:** from about 0.4s (Stamp Slam) to about 1.5s (Spoiler Rewind).
- **When the voice starts:**
  - over the break: Typewriter, Split-Flap;
  - right as it ends: Paper Rip, Stamp Slam, Big Bang, Paper Glitch;
  - after a silence: Record Scratch, Lights Out;
  - after the rewind: Spoiler Rewind.
- **Kinds of hook:** a pure sensory break (Stamp, Big Bang, Lights Out, Glitch), a character break (Knock Knock), a structure break (Spoiler Rewind, Record Scratch) and a rhythm break (Typewriter, Split-Flap).

**For the A/B test:** narrow these to 3–5 openers. Each new reel then draws one at random and records which it used, so performance can be compared opener by opener.

# Batch 2: 10 more openers (2026-10-19)

Built from what the five keepers share:
- **Tactile paper and ink** (Rip, Typewriter): a physical object breaks, not a digital effect.
- **The critter breaking the fourth wall** (Knock Knock).
- **One structural surprise** (the Record Scratch freeze, the Big Bang dot).
- **The voice in by about 1s:** the keepers start it between 0s and 1.0s. The two dropped openers with the latest voice starts (Lights Out at 0.85s, Spoiler Rewind at 1.75s) also held a long silence or a long setup.

Each one is generic: it uses the reel's own scene, critter and title, so it works on any reel. The review cut is again the agent-loop reel.
