## What injection did

- Every builder's system prompt now carries only the core: 51k characters instead of 194k.
- Jev still picked about 34 of the 41 pick sections per window, an average of 121k characters. So
  each builder saw about 172k characters, roughly 11% less than before, not the ~40% the plan hoped
  for.
- Jev does discriminate: the lowest scores were 0.02–0.08. But most sections scored at or above the
  0.35 bar. Raising the bar is the lever.
- There were no misses. No builder Read a section it wasn't given.

Session B fell by about $0.5–1 per reel against the hx6 pair, and turns went down (168 and 187,
against 224 for RL). These are two reels on different topics, so the comparison is indicative, not
proof.

## First-try lessons the ledger has learned (2 renders)

Each lesson is injected into the next builds of that role and look, before their first self-check.

**frame-worker-scene, world**
- Text sits over an object ("view" over "fridge", "6" over "fridge"): move the text to a clear zone,
  or pin it on its own object.
- Promoted to always-inject: `kit:layout-js`, `kit:cards-js`.

**frame-worker-scene, blend**
- Jump cut: the same picture at about 1× size across a cut. Go in to 2× or more, out to 0.5× or
  less, or cut to a new subject.
- Text leaves the safe area (x 96–940, y 570–1600).

**frame-worker-tech, mechanism**
- Jump cut: the same picture at about 1× size across a cut (seen in 02-define and 06-example).
- A UI word runs into an icon or button edge: space them with `layout.js` `row()`/`column()`
  (MIN_GAP).
- Text sits over an object ("how" over "phone").

**scene-builder, world:** its first self-check came back clean in both renders.
