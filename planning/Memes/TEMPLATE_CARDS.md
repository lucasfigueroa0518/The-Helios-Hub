# Template cards: APPROVED (Lucas, 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14)

**Status:** the 60 cards and the exclusions are approved, as written. The
first 25 and the original 9 exclusions: 2026-10-13. The next 35 drafts and
the 7 exclusions from ranks 46+: 2026-10-14. They are in `TEMPLATE_CATALOG`.
A new card or a change needs his approval again.

The classic catalog (ME-50): Imgflip's most-captioned templates, pulled
2026-10-13 (`/get_memes`, ranked by Imgflip). Every image was looked at, not
judged from memory. Of the top 45, **36 pass** the imagery rules and **9 do
not** (see Excluded). Below are cards for the **top 25 that pass**, in rank
order, followed by the 11 other passes as the next batch.

**A card says:**
- **Logic:** the shape a news situation must have to fit (ME-01).
- **Slots:** each place text or a label goes, by position in the image, and
  what kind it is:
  - *entity*: a blue-chip player, rendered by code as an exact name and logo
    (ME-10).
  - *text*: a short line from the story's facts.
  - *viewer*: an approved audience situation's viewer line
    (`AUDIENCE_SITUATIONS.md`).
- **Situations:** the approved situations (AS-xx) it suits.
- **Landscape:** whether it can relate several stories (ME-70).
- **Family:** the Know Your Meme entry that keys its 90-day cooldown (ME-45).
  These are my proposals, confirmed at intake.
- **Burned-in:** text already printed in the image, which we keep and write
  around.
- **Watch:** where it turns wrong, mean or off-rule.

**Rules for every card:**
- A label or logo is placed on or beside a figure and **never covers a face**.
  No face is edited (ME-12).
- Our text is Inter Medium (ME-23), with no Helios branding (ME-24).
- Every claim a slot makes traces to facts (ME-32, ME-74). Framing may
  exaggerate only when the relation underneath is true.
