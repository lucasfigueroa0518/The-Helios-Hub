How the visuals of a Reel AI Drama get built

From pitch to rendered frame, as the code runs today (2026-11-03). Prompts quoted verbatim from explainers/reel-ai-drama/.

Short answer: the director shows what the line says. Its only hard rule about the relationship is that “the line over an image is a claim about it”, so the picture must be what the narration names: the person, the place, the event, the date. Complementing the line is a small, secondary layer: marks, a document the viewer reads, a bridging shot, an inner state. Showing something different from the line is forbidden. Meanwhile the writer is told the opposite: “never narrate what the picture already shows; add context, meaning or consequence.” The two halves don’t agree on whose job is to add and whose is to match.

Directing

Reads the locked script, the inventory and the fact sheet. First plans the best shot per moment as if any real picture existed, then casts each shot from the inventory, a wish (a targeted search), a seeded tool, or a make (drawn or code-built). Composes frames: treatment, assets, motion, marks, sound, music cue, who's shown, labels to drop.
→ plan: frames · makes · wishes · lines_back
↺ lines back: at most one round to the writer when no picture can support a line, then the director re-plans
Wish searchsearch + look
Searches for the shots the director wished for; each wish resolves to the found photo or its fallback.
Facts check · variety checkJev, code
If the script changes or the same person repeats the same way too often, the director re-plans with the problems.
Makesdrawing / code / image model
Builds what no camera took: reconstructed scenes, expressions, collages, drawings.
Building
Storyboard + voicecode + TTS
The plan becomes STORYBOARD.md, one section per frame with each line's label. The narration is voiced and timed word by word.
Integrity checkvision, integrity.md
Looks at each image against the line over it (right person, right era, real artifact). Problems → the director re-plans once.
Frame workersSonnet 5.5, one per frame, frame-worker.md + FRAME.md
Each builds exactly its storyboard frame in code with the kit: images, cutouts, camera moves, marks drawn as the voice says their words, labels on their lines.
Sound design → assemble → rendersound-designer.md
Foley and music cued to the frames; the frames are stitched and rendered to video.
Watching
First viewervision, first-viewer.md
A stranger watches the contact sheet with the voice: when did the hook land, could they follow it on mute, did the hook pull. Picture problems get one fix session (fix.md).
Deliver
The post is written and the pictures used are recorded.

The director's prompt logic on picture vs. narration

MATCHThe rule that governs: the picture is what the line says

“Images show what the line claims, and are what they are used as. The line over an image is a claim about it: the person, the place, the event, the date must be what the image's shows says it shows…”director-craft.md · HARD CONSTRAINT 1
“Check every frame against its lines: does each image (or wish) show what the line claims (the asset's shows, its people, its date)?”director.md · How to work, step 5
“When the narration names a person, show them in that beat (face clear when it's a photo) — because the viewer shouldn't meet a character without looking them in the eye; this holds for every named person, main or not”director-craft.md · §3 Faces and people
“Crop and frame without changing what the picture says, and treat the line over an image as its caption: date, place and event exactly right.”director-craft.md · §9 Image ethics
“For each rung, know what the viewer must see for the rung's purpose to land, and who is in it.”director.md · How to work, step 1

ADDWhere the picture complements the line

“Choose the primary artifact over an illustration of it, and let it carry the narrative — because a real post, page or document is closer to direct experience than any description”director-craft.md · §1
“never a label that repeats what a document in shot visibly says: let the viewer read it (mark, highlight, push in) or add what it doesn't say.”director-craft.md · on-screen words
“Show inner states when the line is about what someone feared, wanted or argued: a real photo where their face carries it… or expression marks drawn around their face.”director.md · Composing the shots
“Bridge pieces of evidence with quiet establishing images — a place, a building, a skyline — so the voice can carry the story across.”director-craft.md · §2 Stills as scenes
“Make abstract claims concrete with an image of the physical thing; show change over time with matched before-and-after frames”director-craft.md · §1

FORBIDDENA picture that says something different

“Don't let juxtaposition assert what the evidence doesn't — never cut to an unrelated image that implies guilt or causation.”director-craft.md · §9 Image ethics
“A generic or mismatched image is never passed off as evidence of the moment”director-craft.md · HARD CONSTRAINT 1

How the story drives the shot (not the inventory)

“Plan the shots first. In script order, decide the best shot for each moment… as if any real photo or document of the record could be had. The story chooses the shot; what the inventory happens to hold never does.”director.md · How to work, step 2
“Build scenes, not slides. A reel of single photos with a push and a label is a slideshow. Give each frame a staging type, and let the type follow the moment”director.md · Composing the shots

What the frame workers may do with it

“Build the storyboard's frame, exactly. … Don't add a beat, an image or a word it doesn't name.”frame-worker.md · HARD CONSTRAINT 1
