# Reel AI Drama: prompt and craft review (2026-10-28)

A read-only review of the craft files, every prompt, `kit/FRAME.md`, and the code that calls them, checked against PLAN (MD-01 to MD-52), flags.json, PROCESS and CALIBRATION. Each finding is one line: `file:line — the problem — the fix`.

Counts: **5 must-fix, 23 should-fix, 12 nit** (40 findings).

## Must-fix

1. `explainers/reel-ai-drama/kit/FRAME.md:50,61` (and `kit/components/treatments.js:447`, `kit/treatment-geometry.js:122`) — the frame-contract example is calibration story #1 (`'$38M'` / `'then he sued.'`), and the word-timing example is `/altman/i`. The director and every frame worker read these. This breaks the CALIBRATION prompt-pollution rule and names a real person, so it can leak into every reel — use neutral placeholders (`'$4M'` / `'then the board met.'`, `/founder/i`, `"$12M"` in the doc comments).
2. `prompts/writer.md:51`, `craft/writer-craft.md:28`, `lib/reel-ai-drama/writing/validate.ts:25` — the close question may run 10 words (about 3.6s at 2.75 words a second). The close is a fixed 2.5s, and `kit/close-timing.js:11` sets `maxQuestion: 2.3` but nothing uses it. A 10-word question either overruns the close or makes the reel longer (S-33, MD-18) — cap it at about 6 words in the prompt, the craft and the validator, and check the voiced question's length against `CLOSE.maxQuestion` after the voice step.
3. `lib/reel-ai-drama/production/run.ts:91,246` — the director's lines back go to `reviseScript` without the inventory. `writer-revise.md:6,15` promises the writer the inventory and needs it to "rewrite so the material in the inventory can carry it", and `validateDraft` then skips the `must_show` checks — add `inventory` to the `reviseScript` input type and pass it at :246 (and at :264).
4. `explainers/reel-ai-drama/kit/project-files.mjs:149`, `prompts/director.md:48-49` — STORYBOARD.md carries only one `on_screen` per frame, so each line's `on_screen` label is lost unless the director copies it. Those labels hold the names, dates and numbers the writer was told carry the muted read (writer-craft §7, W-39), and a frame with 2–3 lines drops all but one — write every carried line's `on_screen` into the storyboard, timed to its line, and have `validatePlan` warn when a label is dropped.
5. `prompts/director.md:70`, `craft/director-craft.md:36`, `writing/validate.ts:201`, `checks/facts.ts:83` — the director may put words "verbatim from the fact sheet" on screen. No code checks that they are verbatim, and the facts check never sees frame `on_screen`, so an on-screen claim can bypass "facts only". It also contradicts PROCESS P5 ("Never decides: the words") — limit frame `on_screen` to the script's line labels and `hook.on_screen`, and have `validatePlan` check that.

## Should-fix

6. `writing/validate.ts:179-225`, `checks/integrity.ts:44` — "one image, one job" (MD-41, MD-46) is a HARD CONSTRAINT, but no code checks it. Also, `harvest/sources/logos.ts:40` marks logos as `character_image: true`, so a logo counts as a "person's character image" — in `validatePlan`, reject `character_image` assets outside `cutout_lift` and `collision`, list non-character assets reused across frames whose purposes differ for the integrity look, and give logos their own flag.
7. `craft/writer-craft.md:273`, `craft/director-craft.md:197-198` — "Most viewers hear the sound … a large minority watch muted" and "the muted minority" go against MD-15 ("Treat most viewers as watching with the sound off") and Lucas's ask that the writing work on mute — restate it as "write as if the sound is off, and reward the viewer who has it on".
8. `craft/writer-craft.md:184,194` — "land the full hook by second 6" and "within the first second or two" contradict HARD 1 (on screen within 1.5s) — delete both, or reword them as "the full stakes are clear by about 6s; the hook itself is on screen by 1.5s".
9. `craft/writer-craft.md:35,153,280,296` vs `prompts/director.md:67`, `writing/validate.ts:197` — the writer budgets "two or three short picture beats" with no narration and "stay quiet while the words show". But every non-close frame must carry a line, and no script field holds a pause, so the beats can't be built — add a `pause_after` field per line (seconds) that pace.mjs honors, or allow a frame with no lines and a set length.
10. `kit/FRAME.md:197` vs `craft/director-craft.md:217,224`, `prompts/director.md:45`, `prompts/frame-worker.md:37` — FRAME treats "shutter" and "door" as sounds that imply an event (they need `shown`, and try.mjs fails them without it). The craft and both prompts list shutter as plain literal foley — drop shutter from the foley examples, or remove it from FRAME's list.
11. `craft/director-craft.md:162` — "Make every frame add new information — repetition stalls the reel" contradicts MD-46 (an image may repeat when it says the same thing again) — reword: "every frame adds something new; an image may return when it means the same thing".
12. `prompts/producer.md:51,56`, `craft/writer-craft.md:108-109` — "protagonist, complication, resolution", "a satisfying end" and "conflict rising toward a resolution" bring back W-2 (cut) and go against MD-05 (the reel ends on the thread still unfolding) — use "the events turn, and there is an outcome or a live open thread".
13. `prompts/producer.md:53-55` — the selection test says "an insight that follows from it … surprise without insight fails". That is lesson creep at the gate, and it isn't one of MD-24's three criteria — replace "insight" with "an implication for the AI world" (MD-24).
14. `prompts/producer.md:58-59` — "Two lines of action, people in conflict, make the strongest stories" pushes every cell toward conflict, though MD-24 and MD-26 widen the field to founding moments, breakthroughs, comebacks and more (wrong altitude) — remove it, or limit it to `rivalry`, `power_struggle` and `courtroom`.
15. `craft/writer-craft.md:219` — "Write one compact, quotable line … usually near the end" invites the reflection-line ending (W-134, cut) and a closing moral (D-6) — limit it to a concrete, fact-bearing line, and never put it in the landing.
16. `craft/writer-craft.md:94,125,126-128` — the "early hinge line that says why the story matters", W-9 ("meaning in at most one line") and D-6 are three overlapping meaning rules, and lesson creep can enter there — merge them into one rule: at most one line of meaning, given as stakes, woven in rather than closing.
17. `prompts/producer.md:120` vs `development/producer.ts:232` — the prompt allows `event_date: null`, but the code drops any pitch without one, which wastes paid candidates — say "always give a date; use `date_precision` year when unsure" in the prompt, or accept null in the code.
18. `prompts/producer.md:20` vs `development/producer.ts:215-292` — "the cell's player is the central figure or at the center" (MD-11) and "`story_type`, `era` — the cell's keys" (:119) aren't checked in code, so a pitch can drift off-cell — in `validatePitch`, require the cell's `player_id` in the cast and the cell's type and era.
19. `reporting/validate.ts:123`, `prompts/writer.md:13-14`, `craft/writer-craft.md` §9 — a key fact with one source is demoted to `single_source`, not killed, and neither the writer prompt nor the craft says what to do with `confidence` — tell the writer to attribute `single_source` claims and give `disputed` ones both sides, or kill the story when a core fact (want, scene, turn) isn't confirmed, as reporting.md:21 requires.
20. `reporting/validate.ts:155` vs `prompts/reporting.md:18` — quotes and artifacts whose page was never fetched are kept with the note "wording unchecked", though the prompt requires a fetched page and the craft shows quotes verbatim — drop them, or mark them `verified: false` so the writer and director never show them as verbatim.
21. `production/run.ts:264-279` — the facts fix rewrites lines after the director planned the picture. `reconcilePlan` only handles cuts, so marks, motion and `on_screen` keyed to the old wording go stale — after a facts revision, re-plan the affected frames through `directReel`'s fix mode, or move the facts check before the director.
22. `production/run.ts:274`, `production/draft.ts:27` vs `prompts/writer-revise.md:31` — the facts cut can remove L1 or the last line, and `hook.on_screen` (plus frame 1's `on_screen`) keeps the hook's promise for a claim that was cut — when the hook line is cut, clear the hook words and flag the reel, and keep the final line's role.
23. `writing/director.ts:15-30`, `craft/director-craft.md:89-91`, `prompts/director.md:28,59` — the director is offered "a drawing" and parallax, but banked drawings and sketches (`ctx.objects`) aren't in its inventory, no harvest step makes the `fg` and `plate` sidecars, and `frame-worker.md:22` forbids assets the inventory doesn't hold. The director doesn't know what he has (Lucas's ask) — list the available drawings, sketches and parallax cuts as inventory assets with ids, or remove those options.
24. `kit/FRAME.md:138-146`, `writing/schemas.ts:23-24` — `lower_third` and `number` are overlays layered on a picture, yet they're frame treatments, and a frame's `treatment` must equal the storyboard's. A plan can't say "photo + number", and a `number` frame needs no asset — remove them from the frame enum and add an `overlay` field (or say they always sit over the frame's first asset).
25. `prompts/visuals-look.md:23-24` — `usable` requires "large enough to fill a phone screen", judged from small Serper thumbnails (`visuals-check.ts:354`), so the pre-scout gate sees false negatives — drop the size test from the look; code already has the dimensions.
26. `prompts/harvest-look.md:6-7` vs `:13` — the caption is "a claim to check, not a fact", yet `shows` may name a person from that caption, and the integrity check then trusts `shows` — write "captioned as X; not confirmed" unless the picture itself identifies them.
27. `checks/first-viewer.ts:75-77` — an empty `want_next`, `keep_watching < 3`, and every `confused` item each trigger a paid picture-only fix session that can't address them — make these warnings, and fix only the hook time, the muted read and the understood-story failures.
28. `prompts/frame-worker.md:32` — "Every move starts with the frame" (S-53, craft) is written as a must and contradicts FRAME's `at` windows, D-53 (time a pan so the detail appears on its line) and the page `arrive` option — change it to "the frame is moving from t = 0; a later move may start on its cue word".
