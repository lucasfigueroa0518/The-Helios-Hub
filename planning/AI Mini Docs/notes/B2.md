# B2 notes: reporting + media harvest (2026-10-28)

## Decisions
- **Reporting** (`reporting/report.ts`): Sonnet 5.5, web_search (10) + web_fetch (15) + strict `submit_fact_sheet` (mirrors `FactSheet`), `tool_choice: auto`, streamed, cached (tools → system), resumes on `pause_turn` by accumulating the assistant turn (up to 4). Every response goes to `.explainers-local/reel-ai-drama/logs/reporting-<job>.jsonl`; cost goes to `recordCost` (component `reporting`), also on failure (`ReportingError.usd`).
- **Fact-sheet checks in code** (`reporting/validate.ts`): duplicate ids are dropped; unknown source ids are stripped; a fact with no real source is dropped, and **a key fact with no source sets `verdict.holds=false`**; a `confirmed` key fact with fewer than two independent sources (different site *and* publisher) becomes `single_source`; timeline entries lose unknown fact ids (dropped if none are left); quotes and artifacts need a real source and must be **word for word on the fetched page** when web_fetch returned it (text from `web_fetch_tool_result`). If the page wasn't fetched, the item is kept and the problem says "unchecked". Problems go to `deps.onProblem` and the log.
- **Text boxes (MD-43)** live in a **sidecar JSON**: `media/V7.boxes.json` = `{ image, width, height, boxes: [{ text, x, y, w, h }] }` in image pixels (one box per wrapped line). The asset carries the flag `boxes:media/V7.boxes.json`. Screenshots are a 540×960 viewport at DPR 2, so 1080×1920 PNGs. The first phrase found is scrolled to 25% of the frame height.
- **Character images:** banked cutouts **and banked logos** are `character_image: true`, so they're exempt from the 20-day rule. A company's logo is its character mark.
- **Trust:** bank, logo bank and our own renders (screens, maps) skip the watermark look. Every image we found goes through the Haiku look; **without a look, a found image is rejected** (`not_checked:no_look`) because the watermark rule is hard (MD-02).
- **Identity:** QID-tied sources (Wikidata P18, Commons depicts, bank) count as verified, provided the look sees a face. Otherwise the caption must name the person and the look must see a face (probable). A person photo that fails is rejected. If the same picture also serves a non-person want, the claim to the person is dropped instead.
- **Recency (MD-09)** never gates. It ranks candidates and adds the flags `old_photo:YYYY` and `later_than_event:YYYY`. `date_basis:` is in the flags.
- **Size:** found images need a short side of at least 600px. Upscaling (MD-16) happens only when the image is the key moment's or the only one for its want, at most 2×. **No upscaler is installed here** (`realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan` is not on PATH), so these images are rejected `too_small` and a note is written.
- **Dedupe:** a 64-bit DCT pHash built with sharp; two images are the same at Hamming ≤ 8. The best copy is kept (trusted > QID > dated > larger). The reuse rule also matches near-copies by pHash.
- **Rejected** candidates get an `assets` row (`status='rejected'`, reason), and their files go to `media/rejected/R#.*`. `media/harvest.json` holds the notes, the rejections and the spend.
- **Licence** is recorded, never filtered (MD-02). The exception is Openverse through the photo finder's `searchOpenverse`, which still applies its own CC allow-list. That is stricter than MD-02, but safe.

## Sources
Implemented: bank, Wikidata P18 + Commons depicts, Commons search, Openverse, Serper (only with a key; capped at 12 queries, priced at $0.001 each), article images (og:image, figures, JSON-LD credits), newsroom/press (official pages from `OFFICIAL_DOMAINS` plus press wires), logo bank, video frames (yt-dlp + ffmpeg), screens (pages, posts, headlines, artifacts), arXiv abstract pages, maps (Nominatim, then B4's `renderMap`).
Skipped or limited:
- **yt-dlp** is not installed here, so video frames are skipped with a note. ffmpeg is installed.
- **PDFs** are not captured yet (no page renderer).
- **Cookie banners** are not dismissed (no autoconsent).
- **Newsroom** reads only the story's own official pages, not company press galleries.
- Not built: Flickr, GDELT, Wayback, Sentinel, EDGAR, CourtListener.

- **B3:** `reporting` prompt: the user message is `Today: YYYY-MM-DD` followed by the pitch as JSON. The output is the `submit_fact_sheet` tool (fields = `FactSheet`). The prompt should say: key facts need two independent sources or a lower confidence; quotes and artifacts are verbatim from a page it **fetched** (searches alone can't be checked); `verdict.holds=false` when the core doesn't hold; cast `qid` when known.
- **B4:** I call `renderMap({ lat, lon, zoom: 15 with an address / 12 without, label, sublabel, out })` and expect it to write a PNG at `out`. `live.ts` imports `explainers/reel-ai-drama/kit/maps/render-map.mjs` at run time and expects a named `renderMap` export. Text boxes: see the sidecar format above.
