## For other builders / the integrator

- **B3 (prompts).** Two prompts are called: `producer` and `visuals-look`, injected as deps (`loadPrompt` / a string), never imported.
  - `producer` sees: system = the prompt, then a JSON roster block `{"blue_chip_players":[{player_id,name,kind}]}` (cached). The user message is JSON: `today`, `cell {player{player_id,name,kind}, story_type{key,label}, era{key,label}, searched_before, reopened_by}`, `candidates_wanted` (2 × kept), `already_pitched` (≤12 fingerprints: title, event_date, central_figure, cast, story_type), `news` (≤10 headlines).
  - The tool is `submit_pitches {candidates: [Pitch + typicality]}`. `typicality` is a 0–1 estimate of how likely another producer would pitch it (verbalized sampling). Code keeps the least typical that pass. The prompt must ask for that number, for the cast's `player_id` to come from the roster (or null), and for a `central_figure` that is in the cast.
  - `visuals-look`: the user message is JSON `{story{title,logline,event_date,cast}, tiles[{index,title,domain}]}`, then "Image i" + an image block per tile. The tool is `submit_look {tiles[{index,usable,watermark,ai_art,relevant,kind|null,note}]}`. Watermark includes TV channel logos (MD-16).
- **B5.** `newsTouching(db, …)` reads the Hub news pool through `lib/stories` (`livePoolReads(db).morning()`). That pool lives on the **shared/stories** connection, not the explainers one. `live.ts` passes the explainers `db`; it should pass the stories DB (or catch the error, since news is optional).
- **B5.** My weekly cap is a rolling 7 days (`developmentSpendSince(now − 7d)`). Your schedule uses a Monday-NY week. Both are fine; say if you want me to take a `weekStart` option.
- **Jev versions.** `QuestionSet.version` is a string, but `jev_logs.question_set_version` is `integer`. My sets use ids `reel-ai-drama-{drama,material,same-event}-v1` with `version: '1'`. A non-numeric version would fail the insert (worth telling every Jev user).

## Decisions I made

- **Ledger.** I don't seed company cells in eras that ended before the company was founded (e.g. Anthropic before 2019). That saves empty producer calls. 43 players give 2,758 cells. `pickCells` goes in this order: tiers (new → due → reopened), then spread passes (one cell per player and per type → relax the type → relax the player). A reopen needs `since` (the news date) so a news item reopens a cell only once.
- **Dedupe.**
  - `eventKey` = central figure | sorted cast slugs | story_type | month (year at year precision).
  - Similarity = 0.3 × title+scene words, 0.45 × cast, 0.25 × date closeness.
  - At 0.85 or above it is a duplicate with no model call. From 0.45 up to 0.85 it is borderline, and Jev pairs run in batches of 5 (at most 10 pairs).
  - Pitches with status `duplicate` are excluded from the history. The thresholds are a first guess; tune them on labelled pairs.
- **Scores.**
  - Drama: `central_want` 0.4, then stakes, scene, turn and implication at 0.15 each.
  - If `central_want` is under 2/4 the score is capped at 0.45. A "mostly spectacle" yes (noul ≥ 0.5) caps it at 0.5. Both caps fall under the 0.7 bar.
  - Material: faces .25, photographed events .2, artifacts .2, variety .15, place .1, era .1.
  - Neither set sees the producer's expected_visuals or sources (no self-grading).
- **Visuals check.**
  - The gates are research 04's G1 to G3. Green = all three pass. Red = G2 misses, or G1 and G3 both miss. Amber = anything else. I dropped research 04's points score.
  - A company as the central figure needs a banked logo instead of a face.
  - Without the Haiku look, image hits count only if their title names the cast, at half weight.
- **Pool.**
  - rank = 0.85 × drama + 0.15 × material, +0.05 for fresh news with a why-now, −0.03 for amber.
  - "News" means the event was inside `news_window_days` when the pitch was made; anything older is history and never fades.
  - News past the window with no why-now fades (×0.5), and `tendPool` retires it at 2 windows with a revive condition.
  - `tendPool` returns `{faded, retired, rechecked}`, where `rechecked` = pooled rows re-ranked. It does not re-run visuals checks, since those cost money and `tendPool` has no deps.
- **Session.** It handles pending `new` pitches first (seeds, or leftovers from a capped session). `trigger:'calibration'` only handles those, with no cells. Failed producer calls are not marked searched. Every reject gets a reason and a revive condition.
