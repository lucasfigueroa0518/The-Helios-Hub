# B5 notes: Orchestration and lab (2026-10-28)

## Decisions
- **Spend.** Builders record their own calls with `recordCost` under the job id I pass them (B2 `reportStory` already does this). After every step, `runJob` settles the ledger's new spend into one `SpendMeter` on `per_reel_cap_usd`. Agent sessions are metered live by `runAgentSession`. I record HeyGen voice myself with `usd: null`. Passing the cap fails the job as capped, adds a `cost` flag and puts the pitch back in the pool.
- **Kills.** `facts_dont_hold`, `too_few_visuals` and `no_key_moment_image` set the job to `killed` and the pitch to `killed` (reason in `reject_reason`). Then `nextForProduction` runs and a new job is queued with the same trigger, for clicks too. A harvest with `usable_count < min_visuals` kills even when `inventory.kill` is null.
- **Failures.** Any failure, capped or not, puts the pitch back to `pooled`. `weeklyTick` counts failed jobs in the week's `per_week`, so retries stay within the cadence. Killed jobs do not count: the replacement takes the slot.
- **Weekly clock.** The week starts Monday 00:00 New York. Development runs once a week: no `trigger='auto'` session yet this week and dev spend under the cap → `tendPool`, then one session. Slot i opens at weekStart + i×7/per_week days.
- **Calibration report.** There is no table for it. It is kept at `.explainers-local/reel-ai-drama/calibration/latest.json`, written by the calibrate route and script.
- **Agent session overrides** (`production/agents.ts`, copied from Real Case, not imported):
  - `frame-worker` gets our `frame-worker` prompt.
  - Removed: scene-builder, frame-worker-scene, frame-worker-tech, reader, diagnoser, first-viewer.
  - The picture-checker is kept.
