# Real Case Reels — decisions register

**Now "AI News Explained" (RC-26):** a recent, true AI news story told as a story, with the concept
learned along the way. Format spec: `FORMAT.md`. Entries marked *superseded* below are kept for history.

A second route alongside Explainer Reels: a true, checkable real-world story shows the concept at
work, and the reel explains the concept through it. Background:
`.explainers-local/previews/format-samples.md` (Format 3), `planning/Explainer Reels/3.8 checkpoint/`.

Local only until a run validates it: no commits to main, no deploys, no Supabase apply without Lucas's OK.
