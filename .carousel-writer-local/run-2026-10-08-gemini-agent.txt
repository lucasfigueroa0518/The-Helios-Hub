CAPTION:
Google Cloud has announced the Gemini agent, an AI agent that Google says can take on tasks for employees across their work apps. It is in private preview for enterprise customers inside the Gemini Enterprise app, with consumers to follow later.

An AI agent is software that takes ownership of assigned tasks instead of only answering questions. Google says this one answers questions, handles knowledge work, creates images and media, and writes and runs code from one interface. CEO Thomas Kurian announced it at the Gemini at Work 2026 event. Google says the agent can be given its own Workspace account and email address, and that every action it takes is logged to an audit trail under the agent's name. Spend caps pause a project's agent when the budget is hit. Pricing for the agent has not been given.

Meta introduced Muse in September and OpenAI introduced Dots in late September, CBS reports. No independent hands-on reviews of Google's agent were found, so what it can do comes from Google.
