## Caption

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5 on October 7, 2026, a small model priced at $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens. Haiku 4.5 cost $1.00 and $5.00.

Anthropic says the new model costs around 75% less to run on average and beats OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna on every row of its own benchmark table where Luna has a score. Sonnet 5.5 still scores ahead of Haiku 5.5 in that table, and Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 remain better for complex agentic coding. Kingy AI reads the system card’s strict pass rate on the OSWorld 2.1 computer-use test at 37.1%, against the 72.4% partial-credit score Anthropic highlights. The Decoder says the tokenizer change means real savings are likely smaller than per-token prices suggest. No independent benchmark of Haiku 5.5 was found.
