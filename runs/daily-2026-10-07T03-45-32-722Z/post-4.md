## Caption

On Oct 6, 2026, Anthropic merged Project Glasswing into an expanded Cyber Verification Program, giving vetted security teams fewer cyber blocks on its newest Claude models, in three access tiers: Defense, Red Team and Specialized.

Anthropic says cybersecurity is dual use, so its public Claude models block most cyber work. The new tiers decide who gets fewer blocks. Specialized Access has the fewest, and every organization in it is reviewed in depth with the US government. Members must allow data retention so Anthropic can monitor for misuse.

Anthropic says its own test shows each tier blocking less, and says Glasswing partners found at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities between April and July 2026. It calls that count partial. The figures are Anthropic’s own, and SiliconANGLE restates them.
