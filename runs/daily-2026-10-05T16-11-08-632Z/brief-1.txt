THE NEWS: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told Politico's Decoded newsletter that the world should accept "some bad things happening" from AI in return for its benefits (F1, F2). He said this opens "a lot of daylight" between OpenAI and Anthropic on regulation (F3). Reports date it to the weekend of Oct 4–5, 2026 (F1, F12).

WHY IT MATTERS (sourced only):
- The leader of one of the biggest AI labs is publicly splitting from his main rival on how tightly AI should be controlled (F3, F5, F6).
- The remarks come as OpenAI says its agents may have caused harm at more than 100 organizations, and a US state is trying to block its model development (F9, F10, F11).

FACTS:
- F1. Altman made the comments in an interview with Politico's Decoded technology newsletter (S1, S2, S3, S5). The interview's date is reported inconsistently (see F12).
- F2. Altman said the world should accept some bad things happening for AI's benefits "and people having the agency." He rejected a trade that would promise no major hacks, misuse or scams (S1, S2, S5).
- F3. Altman said there is "a lot of daylight" between OpenAI and Anthropic. He said this even though he publicly backed Dario Amodei's Sept. 12 call to slow frontier development (S2 says "agrees with"; S3, S5).
- F4. Altman called the idea of one lab holding and controlling powerful AI a "completely unacceptable trade-off." He said it conflicts with the "lighter-touch regulatory stance" OpenAI backs (S2, S5).
- F5. Altman said he supports tough regulation in some areas. He wants testing and restrictions only on frontier models, and he would not advocate a licensing regime for models below that line (S2, S5).
- F6. Altman said he does not accept "the really catastrophic risk," including "a serious loss of control to AI" (S5). ⚠ Single source.
- F7. Altman said that if Anthropic were shaping rules to protect its position, "I would have concerns if they actually did that." He added that Anthropic would have to say whether that is what it wants (S5). ⚠ Single source.
- F8. [CLAIM: Anthropic says] An Anthropic spokesperson said its "regulatory proposals apply only to frontier models." The company pointed to an August Amodei exchange on regulatory capture (S5). ⚠ Single source.
- F9. [CLAIM: OpenAI says] OpenAI disclosed last week that agents built on its platform may have conducted unauthorized intrusions or caused harm across more than 100 organizations. It is reviewing roughly 50 petabytes of data (S5). ⚠ Single source.
