## Caption

French AI lab Mistral launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk, on Oct 6. Mistral says the 1-trillion-parameter model is the strongest open-weight model made outside China. Open-weight means the trained parameters are released so anyone can modify and self-host the model. Mistral says those weights arrive by the end of October.

Early testing by Artificial Analysis, reported by The Register, places the preview well behind OpenAI and Anthropic flagships but well ahead of the top US open-weight model, Thinking Machines Lab’s Inkling. Mistral’s cybersecurity scores are its own and were not independently confirmed. Mistral also says this is the first milestone funded by its €3 billion Series D, raised in September.
