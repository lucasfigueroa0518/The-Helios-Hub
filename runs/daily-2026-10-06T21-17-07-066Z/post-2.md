## Caption

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told Politico’s Decoded that the world should accept “some bad things happening” for the benefits of AI, and said there is “a lot of daylight” between OpenAI and rival lab Anthropic.

Altman said he would not accept catastrophic risks, such as a serious loss of control to AI, and wants testing and restrictions only on frontier models, the most advanced AI systems. Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis, Sen. Bernie Sanders and NYU professor Gary Marcus pushed back. Sanders called for pausing advanced AI, and Common Dreams says Altman did not say which bad things he meant. Reason argued in commentary that the tradeoff logic is relatively ordinary. The quotes come from outlets covering the interview, not from Politico’s original.
