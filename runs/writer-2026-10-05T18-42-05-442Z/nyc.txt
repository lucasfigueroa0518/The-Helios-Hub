Senior executives from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta testified under oath at a rare New York City Council hearing on AI risks on Oct 5, 2026. Elon Musk's SpaceXAI did not show up despite a subpoena, a legal order compelling attendance and sworn testimony.

The hearing convened all 51 Council members. The companies sent policy leads, not CEOs. Speaker Julie Menin's office says OpenAI, Google and Anthropic agreed to appear only after the Council threatened subpoenas. Menin says SpaceXAI's absence violates its subpoena and that the Council will pursue the matter in court. Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon called the industry's approach extremely reckless. The Council is weighing proposals that include outside-expert vetting of AI and a kill switch, with a $25,000 fine for violating either of those two laws. They are proposals only. Menin opened by criticising the federal government's light-touch approach.
