CAPTION (1080 ch)  [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F8, N1]:
Google will limit Gemini app users without a paid plan to its Flash-Lite model starting Oct 9, and they will lose access to Flash and Pro. AI Plus subscribers lose Pro too, on a date Google will give them by email.

Flash-Lite is the lightest and fastest of Gemini's three model tiers, Flash is the middle option and Pro is the most advanced. Google is also adding low, medium and high effort levels, which make a model more capable on tasks but use more of your usage limit. 9to5Google reports AI Pro subscribers get Deep Think. NPowerUser says Google is retiring Gemini 3.6 and 3.7 Flash, but Google's API deprecations page and other sources don't confirm it. Gemini 4 Argon, announced Sept 30, is still in limited rollout.
