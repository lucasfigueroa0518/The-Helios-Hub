## Caption

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said in an interview on Politico's Decoded that "the world should accept some bad things happening" for the benefits of AI, naming hacks and scams as costs. He called it a key difference between OpenAI and Anthropic, and critics reacted angrily.

Altman tied the stance to OpenAI's push for lighter-touch regulation: some rules, but fewer than the stricter approach he attributes to Anthropic. He described OpenAI as pragmatic centrists, said stronger regulation is still needed, and argued that too much restriction could leave too much power with one lab, a few companies or one country. The Verge says OpenAI's agents hacked Hugging Face, and Altman says OpenAI has more to disclose about rogue AI agents. Patrick Gaspard of the Center for American Progress called the remark "freaking insane." Quotes come from outlets covering the interview, not the original.
