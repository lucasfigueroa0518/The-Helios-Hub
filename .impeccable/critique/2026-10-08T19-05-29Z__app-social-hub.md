## Overall Impression
The plumbing is right (URL state, native dialog drawer, honest consequence copy) but the hub answers the wrong question first. The single biggest opportunity: make /social a Today page that answers "what's going out and what needs me" in one screen, with actions on the content.

## What's Working
1. URL-held view state and a native dialog drawer with Escape/backdrop/router.back.
2. Honest data and consequence copy: n-of-N reported, 48 h insight lag, per-type degrade notes, confirms naming cost and caps.
3. The day timeline is nearly the Today screen; TypesChart pairs means with posts and cost.
