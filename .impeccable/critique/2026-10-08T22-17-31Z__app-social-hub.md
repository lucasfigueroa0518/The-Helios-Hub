## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Not slop. One Signal Rule enforced in code (approval-off rows go quiet). Borderline tells: four identical pool cards, a sparkline in every KPI tile.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI 2 advisory design-system findings (2px heat-swatch radius off scale; `#000` inside a mask gradient, a false positive since masks use alpha only); 0 with `--no-config`. Browser: tiny-text, thin-border-shadow, cramped-padding, clipped thead, Roboto all confirmed false positives. True positives: out-of-month calendar text at 2.12:1 (opacity 0.55 on subtle text), photo credits truncated on phones (licence text), pool "next" items clipped 2–11px, three prose lines over 75ch. ## Priority Issues

**[P1] Calendar type toggles do the opposite of the click.** From All, clicking a type hides it. Fix: from All, a click isolates that type; after that, clicks add and remove. (/impeccable polish)

**[P1] Needs-you counts disagree.** Header 4, groups 28, Oct 9 badge 3 vs 2 sheet rows (ready posts counted on the calendar but not listed in the sheet); a ready post "Made tomorrow". Fix: count only placed posts per day; say "N later" in the header; never print a future made time. (/impeccable harden)

**[P1] Red states with no way forward.** Late has no link or next step and the drawer says "Posts Today 11:59 AM" above it; the stale-pool alarm on Content looks normal on the pool page. Fix: Late says what to check and links there; stale alert carried into the pool page. (/impeccable clarify)

**[P2] Phone buries today.** Approval-off rows and a 4-line note sit above "Going out today". Fix: fold approval-off rows into one collapsed group with the explanation. (/impeccable adapt)

**[P2] Pipeline vocabulary and a wrong gloss.** Fix: "Judge score", "Idea judge: …", drop row-constant and raw status details, short dates, per-type Origin gloss, verdict sentences that read as sentences. (/impeccable clarify)
