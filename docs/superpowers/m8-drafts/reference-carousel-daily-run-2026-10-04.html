One day, end to end

Sunday, Oct. 4, 2026. The new design run on one real news day: feeds in, 10 candidates scored by Jev, 2 winners, and each winner taken through Reporter, Writer, Editor, Fact-checker and Design to a finished carousel waiting for Publish.

Read this as a walkthrough of the design, not output from the built system. The rebuild is still being coded. Every stage below was carried out by hand following the spec's rules, using real articles and sources. Jev's yes/no answers are stand-in judgments. Photos are shown as placeholders labelled with the source the photo lookup would use. Quotes and numbers are copied exactly from the sources.

Updated after review
  • No more than 2 slides in a row with the same look. The renderer now rotates layouts (full photo, photo on top, text on top, stat, quote, spread). Each slide shows its layout in small print at the bottom left.
  • The cover arrow moved to the top right, set a little lower so Instagram's "1/N" slide counter doesn't cover it. The headline stays bottom-left and can never run into it.
  • Photos are never stretched. They're scaled evenly and cropped to fit, and a check compares the rendered shape with the original file. The grey blocks here are placeholders, not real photos.

Step 2 · Jev scoring

10 candidates, scored on yes/no questions

Required: AI is the main subject (A) and there's enough for 5–8 slides (S). Adds to score: a number or quote (M), why it matters is clear (W), a primary source or several outlets (P), a photographable named subject (Ph). Skip list: weapons and war, deaths and tragedies, crime and violence, sexual abuse and deepfake porn. Ties go to the story with more outlets.
