# Idea pool standard: one set of rules for every content type (2026-10-21)

**Status:** draft for Tommy's review. Every product decision below was made by Tommy in the 2026-10-21 interview unless marked **(my call)**.
**Branch:** `unify-social-hub` (no new branches).
**Folds in:** [2026-10-21-idea-seeding-design.md](2026-10-21-idea-seeding-design.md). Where the two conflict, this document wins; the conflicts are listed under "Changes to the seeding spec".

## Why

Each content type runs its idea pool a little differently, and the differences now bite: Generate on an Explainers idea can answer with an error, there is no way to reorder the queue by hand, the post shown on top is not part of the queue, and "delete" throws work away. This document sets one standard for how a pool is capped, ordered, pinned, generated and archived, for Reels (Text on Screen), Explainers, Carousels, Stories and Last Few Days. Explainers is built first and proves the standard; the others follow.

## Goals

- One pool vocabulary and one set of controls on every type's idea pool and Content page.
- Tommy can reorder the queue with a drag and decide what the night makes.
- Any unpublished idea can always be generated by a human click.
- Nothing is ever hard-deleted from the front end; Archive is a soft delete.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- **No sweeping (CLAUDE.md Rule 2).** Capping, archiving and ordering act on rows the pool already holds, one action at a time or in the nightly refill the type already runs. Nothing here enriches, backfills or iterates the database to spend money.
- **No live model calls from tests or from agents (Rule 1).** Every generate path that costs money starts with a human click and shows its cost first.
- Anti-goal: a hand move that silently does nothing (the night ignoring a drag), or a machine step that silently undoes one the person still expects.
- Not in this document: restoring archived ideas (no Restore button), changing how scores are computed, the seeding forms per type (see the seeding spec).

## The model

### Lifecycle and flags **(my call)**

An idea has one lifecycle state and any number of flags. "Manually adjusted" is not a state; it is derived from the flags.

| | Values |
|---|---|
| **Lifecycle** | Idea (scored, nothing made) → Generated (content exists, unpublished) → Published. Or **Archived** from Idea or Generated. |
| **Flags** | **Pinned**, **Placed** (dragged by hand), **Yours** (seeded; the seeding spec's `origin = 'manual'`) |

The pool shows chips for Pinned, Placed and Yours, so a person can see why an idea sits where it does.

### Order

Top to bottom: **pinned ideas** (in the order pinned), then **everything else by effective score**. An idea's effective score is its machine score, or, if it has been dragged, its **placement key** (the number it was dropped at, below). A placed idea is not a separate group: it sits among the others at the spot it was dropped. The machine score is never rewritten. The placement key is stored as an overlay (see Data), so the nightly rescore cannot overwrite it and the "Where the score comes from" breakdown stays true.

**The key is absolute (my call).** At drop, the key is set to 0.01 above (or below) the neighbour's *effective score as displayed at that moment* (if the neighbour is itself placed, its key). It does not follow the neighbour afterwards: if a rescore moves the neighbour before tonight's making, the placed idea keeps its number and may drift a place or two. Tommy chose that a placement expires after tonight rather than holding a fixed slot, so a small drift is acceptable. Dropping into a gap smaller than 0.01 uses the midpoint.

### The queue is the Content page

The Content page's top area shows the **top N ideas of the queue, where N is the type's daily quota** (Reels 3, Explainers 2, Carousels 2, Last Few Days 1, Stories by series). Each of the N slots shows that idea's generated post, or an empty slot with "Generate". #1 is simply the first slot. The post shown on top is no longer separate from the queue; it is the queue's top entries rendered with their content.

Stories rank within each series and never across series (existing behavior, `bySeries`); a Stories "queue" is one per series and the rules below apply per series. **N for a series is the number of sets it builds that night (one when the series is due, none otherwise) (my call);** on the Content page each series shows its slot in the series order the page already uses.

### Drag

- Drag any open pool row to a new position (handle on the row, smooth glide, drop preview, like a music queue). It also works from the keyboard (handle focus, arrow keys, confirm), and on a phone width.
- **Where it lands:** the dropped idea sorts just above the idea below it, or equivalently 0.01 above the next-ranked idea beneath it in the displayed order. A drag from 15 to 5 lands 0.01 above the idea now at 6; a drag from 5 to 14 lands 0.01 below the idea now at 13. This is the Placed overlay's sort key; the machine score is untouched.
- **Drag above the pins:** the idea becomes **Pinned** and is placed first. While dragging, the pin zone lights up with a label ("Drop here to pin") so it is never a surprise. Dropping there is a pin and follows the pin rules, including the cost prompt.
- **Drag a pinned idea below the last pin:** it **unpins** and takes the position where it was dropped.
- **Drag into the top N** (a generated idea out, an ungenerated one in): the displaced idea **keeps its content and sinks**; nothing is archived. If the idea that lands in the top N has no content, the app asks "Generate this now? About $X" with a "Not now" option (Rule 1: that confirm is the human click).
- **Stories:** a drag stays inside its series.

### Placement expires **(Tommy: steers tonight, then resets)**

A placement **steers tonight's making** (the night makes the top N of the queue as ordered), then **expires**: after that type's nightly run, the idea competes by score again. A placement is also consumed early when its idea is made. A pin does not expire this way; it lasts until the idea is made or published, or until the person unpins it (by dragging it down or an Unpin action).

**When "after the run" counts (my call):** a placement expires when that type's nightly run **completes with auto-render on** (every top-N slot was made, or skipped with a recorded reason). If the run fails, does not run, or auto-render is off, the placement stays, so a hand move is never undone by a step that did nothing for it. A placement that outlives **7 days** expires anyway, so it cannot sit forever. Stories expire per series, when that series' set for the night has been built.

**Ideas that drop out (my call):** a pin, placement or archive on an idea that is absent from the pool (a Carousels story that left the shortlist) is ignored while absent, as `applyMoves` does today, and does not count toward the 30. It comes back into force if the idea reappears within the 7 days, then is cleaned up. Archive tombstones never expire.

### Pin

- Any number of pins. They hold the top positions in the order pinned.
- Pin replaces Promote on the pool. Demote is dropped as a user control **(my call: nothing in the new model uses it; the stored kind is kept for old rows)**.
- A pin is cleared when its idea is made or published.
- **Cost:** pinning an idea with no content asks "Generate this now? About $X" with "Not now", which keeps the pin without generating. A pin never starts a render by itself.
- With auto-render off, a pin on an ungenerated idea waits for a Generate click; the pin says so.

### Generate

- **Every unpublished idea can be generated, always.** This fixes the Explainers error, where `requestRender` refuses unless the topic's status is `pool`, `promoted` or `queued` (`topic_not_renderable`, [lib/explainers/repository.ts](../../../lib/explainers/repository.ts) `RENDERABLE`). Every other unpublished status becomes generatable by click.
- **Caps ask instead of block.** If the daily render cap or the daily spend cap is hit, Generate shows the cost and asks "Go over the cap?". The click authorizes that one render. A render already running for the idea shows its progress instead of an error.
- **The cap is soft for a human click, hard for the night (my call).** The confirmed request carries an explicit override flag the server honors for that one render only; the spend is recorded in the same cost ledger as any render, so the day's totals stay true. The nightly worker still honors both caps. The "About $X" estimate comes from each type's recorded averages (as the seeding spec does for Explainers), with a constant as the fallback.
- **Regenerate on an approved or scheduled post (Tommy):** the post is **unscheduled and unapproved** at once, and the old version can no longer hold the #1 slot or any queue slot as the content to publish. The new version becomes the idea's current content and is what the UI shows everywhere (cards, Content page, pool). It needs Approve again. Nothing can publish content that was not reviewed after the regenerate.
- **Versions (Tommy):** an idea with several generated versions shows a version toggle in its detail view, so a person can look back at a prior version, including the one that had been scheduled. The pool already counts versions (`versionCount`). **Viewing only is in scope (my call):** making an older version current again is not part of this design; if wanted, it would be a new Generate-free "Use this version" action that also needs Approve.
- Generating an idea that already has content is Regenerate: a new version, per each type's existing versioning.

### Cap: 30 open ideas per type

- "Open" means waiting plus content-ready. Stories cap per series.
- Overflow drops the **lowest-scored machine idea** (status archived by the system, not shown). **Pinned, Yours, Placed and Generated ideas are never dropped**, so the real maximum can exceed 30. This supersedes the seeding spec's trim exemption and its separate "10 open seeds" cap, except that the 10-seed cap stays **(my call)** as a guard against a runaway. It counts **open** seeds: archiving or making one frees a place.
- Explainers' `pool_size` setting (default 25, no max) is capped at 30 and its UI states the limit. Other types get the same ceiling in code.
- The cap is enforced in each type's nightly refill and on seed or add, so a pool can never grow past it silently.

### Archive

- **Archive replaces Reject and Delete.** On an idea in the pool and on a generated post's card. It is a soft delete: hidden from the front end, kept in the back end.
- Archived ideas are **remembered so the nightly refills do not bring them back** (see Data). There is no Restore button in this version.
- Archiving a **scheduled** post unschedules it first. A **published** post cannot be archived.
- The Content card's buttons become **Approve, Regenerate, Archive**.

### Overnight behavior

Each type's night worker walks the queue **top-down** (pins, then placements, then score) and makes whatever has no content, within the usual caps and only where auto-render is on. Content already made does **not** jump the queue (this replaces the "made content wins the quota" rule in [lib/social-hub/views/day-rank.ts](../../../lib/social-hub/views/day-rank.ts)); if the top N already have content, there is nothing to make. A pin or placement the night cannot make records a skip reason (existing `recordPromoteSkip` path) and the worker moves down the queue.

## Data and code **(my call, the builder may improve it)**

- **Reuse `social_hub.idea_adjustments`** ([lib/social-hub/moves-store.ts](../../../lib/social-hub/moves-store.ts)); the four night workers already read it. Add kinds `pin`, `place` (with a sort key) and `archive`, plus an `unpin` or a consumed row for removal. Promote rows are read as pins; Demote rows are left alone and ignored by the new order. An active Promote becomes a pin that lasts until made, instead of one a night; there are few of them, and this is the intended change.
- **No backfill (my call).** Archive tombstones apply to ideas archived after launch. Earlier Reject and Delete outcomes are already kept out of the pool by each type's existing statuses and dedupe (for example Explainers' `rejected_*`, `archived` and history check); the builder verifies that for each type and reports any gap rather than sweeping rows (Rule 2).
- **Archive memory:** a tombstone per `(vertical, source key)` that each type's refill checks before inserting. Keys: Explainers topic id and normalised title; Reels idea id; Carousels story id; Stories pool key; Last Few Days headline key. Stories' refill deletes unused rows it did not return, so it needs the tombstone check to avoid resurrecting an archived row.
- **Ordering functions:** extend `applyMoves`/`orderTonight` ([lib/social-hub/views/moves.ts](../../../lib/social-hub/views/moves.ts)) so the Pool page, the Content page, Run now and all four workers share one pure ordering. It stays free of Next, Jev and a database client so workers can import it.
- **Types without an idea table:** Carousels' pool is the `shortlist` inside a daily `social.runs` record, so pins, placements and archive attach to the story id in `idea_adjustments`, not to a row. Last Few Days' pool is the latest post's queue.
- **Content page:** [lib/content-type/model.ts](../../../lib/content-type/model.ts) `typeHubModel` builds days from posts and a separate bench; the top-N-is-the-queue change touches how it builds `days[today]` and `bench`.
- **UI:** drag handle and drop preview in `PoolScreen`, pin-zone highlight, chips, cost confirm dialog, Pin/Unpin/Archive actions replacing `RankButtons`.
- **Worker sync:** ordering, archive checks and the cap are worker logic for four types. Redeploy `helios-orch-worker` via `./scripts/gcp/deploy-worker-code.sh` and `helios-social-worker` via `./scripts/gcp/deploy-social-worker.sh` in the same session, daytime only.

## Rollout

1. **Shared core:** pure ordering and cap functions, new adjustment kinds, tombstones, API routes, the drag/pin/archive UI components, cost-confirm dialog.
2. **Explainers** end to end, including the Generate fix and the worker change. Proven here first.
3. **Reels (Text on Screen)**, then **Stories**, then **Last Few Days**, then **Carousels** (no idea table, heaviest). Each step ends with its worker redeploy.

## Changes to the seeding spec

- Seeds are not trimmed (kept) but now count toward the 30. Pinned, Yours, Placed and Generated ideas are exempt from overflow dropping.
- A seed lands by score and competes with the machine's ideas; it is **not** bumped to the top (Tommy confirmed).
- "Pin" there (the Promote rename, one per night) is replaced by the pin defined here (many, ordered, held until made).
- Removal of a seed uses Archive, not a custom Remove.
- Seeding never starts a render; the post-seed toast's "Make it now" is the same cost-confirm as any Generate.

## Tests (offline; no live model)

- Ordering: pins, then placements, then score; a drop lands 0.01 above/below the right neighbour for moves up and down, with ties and adjacent placements.
- Drag above the pins pins; drag a pin below the last pin unpins and keeps the drop position.
- A placement is consumed after the type's nightly run has made its picks, and when its idea is made; a pin is not.
- Cap: 31st machine idea drops the lowest-scored; pinned, Yours, Placed and Generated survive; Stories cap is per series.
- Archive: a tombstoned key is skipped by each type's refill; archiving a scheduled post unschedules it; a published post refuses.
- Generate: every unpublished status is accepted; a hit cap returns an "ask" result, not a refusal; the confirmed retry succeeds.
- Night worker: walks top-down, makes only empty slots, skips and records a reason when a cap blocks.
- Manual, by Tommy in the browser: drag, pin with the cost prompt, archive, and Generate on an Explainers idea that used to error.
